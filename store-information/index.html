Shopping with EZRefill

This site is a product showcase. Purchases, activation, and service arrangements are handled with the store. Contact us to confirm stock, color, carrier compatibility, final product pricing and applicable product taxes before purchasing.

Prepaid plans

Plan prices include taxes and fees and cover the stated prepaid term. Data allowances renew each month. T-Mobile Prepaid offers Unlimited for 3 months at $120. H2O offers 4, 8, 12 and 40 GB plans for 4 months. Ultra Mobile and Lycamobile offer the displayed 6-month plans and 3-month Unlimited at $155. Ask the store about coverage, speeds, usage policies and activation requirements for your chosen carrier.

Products & illustrations

Listed iPhones are new. Colors and 3D imagery illustrate the model and may differ from available stock. The exploded iPhone animation is an artistic product visualization, not repair instructions. Chargers and cables shown are unbranded accessories, not Apple-made chargers.

Returns, warranties & service

Ask the store for the return, exchange and warranty terms that apply to your purchase before paying. This website does not add a separate warranty or promise a particular return period.
