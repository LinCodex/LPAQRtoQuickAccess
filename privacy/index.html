PRIVACY & YOUR CHOICES

Privacy, plainly.

Updated September 10, 2026.

A simple store website

This website showcases EZRefill products and services. It has no online checkout, account registration, contact form, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts.

Your language choice

We use your browser language to choose English or Chinese. If you change it, we save only that preference in your browser’s local storage, under ezrefill-language. It is not sent to us or used to track you. You can switch it at any time or remove it by clearing this site’s browser data.

Cookies & media

The website application does not set cookies. Product images, 3D models, and code are served from this site. We use system fonts, so viewing a page does not contact a font provider. Your browser may cache files to make future visits faster.

Connections to the website

Like any website, our hosting provider receives connection information such as an IP address, browser information, and the requested page when you visit. Any hosting security logs are separate from the application described above.

Calling & directions

Calling opens your phone service. The directions link opens Google Maps only when you choose it. Those providers handle your information under their own policies. We do not embed a map or send them your visit automatically.
